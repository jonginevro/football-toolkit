Game-day weather

The forecast for kickoff through the next few hours at every stadium, and what those conditions usually do to kickers, passing games and the run.

What the conditions usually mean

ConditionUsual effect
Wind 15+ mph or gusts 28+Long field goals and deep passes suffer first. At 20+ mph teams tend to run more and totals drop.
RainA wet ball means more drops and fumbles. Passing volume and efficiency dip a little; heavy rain matters more.
SnowFooting and visibility drop. Scoring usually falls and kickers struggle.
Cold (32°F and below)Kicks travel shorter; in bitter cold the ball is harder to throw and catch.
Dome or closed roofNo effect.

Forecasts reach about 16 days out and get more reliable closer to kickoff. They refresh every day.
